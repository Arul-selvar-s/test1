What Is Prosthodontics?

Prosthodontics is the dental speciality focused on restoring and replacing teeth. Whether you have damaged teeth, missing teeth, or need a complete smile makeover, prosthodontic treatment rebuilds your mouth to full function and natural appearance.

At Elysian Dental Lounge in Kovilambakkam, Chennai, Dr. Yokesh Kumar R provides comprehensive prosthodontic care — from a single dental crown to complete full-mouth rehabilitation — using the latest materials and techniques.

Types of Prosthodontic Treatments We Offer

Our prosthodontic services cover the full range of restorative options, tailored to your specific needs and budget.

  • Dental Crowns — Custom-made porcelain or ceramic caps that cover a damaged tooth, restoring its shape, size, and strength
  • Dental Bridges — Fixed restorations that replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent natural teeth
  • Dentures — Removable complete or partial dentures crafted for a natural look and comfortable fit
  • Implant-supported Restorations — Crowns, bridges, and dentures supported by dental implants for maximum stability
  • Full Mouth Rehabilitation — Comprehensive treatment combining multiple procedures to restore the entire mouth

Why Restoring Damaged Teeth Matters

Many patients delay prosthodontic treatment thinking it is cosmetic. In reality, damaged or missing teeth cause serious problems over time — neighbouring teeth shift into the gap, bite becomes uneven, jaw bone begins to shrink, and facial structure changes.

Early treatment always leads to simpler, more affordable solutions. A single crown placed today prevents a much more complex procedure next year.

Dr. Yokesh Kumar's advice: Never ignore a cracked or broken tooth. What looks minor on the surface is often progressing underneath. Early intervention saves both the tooth and your budget.
