What Is Clearalign?

Clearalign is a modern orthodontic system that uses a series of custom-made, virtually invisible removable trays to gradually move your teeth into their ideal position. Each aligner is worn for 1–2 weeks before progressing to the next in the series — gently and precisely guiding your smile.

Unlike traditional braces, Clearalign leaves no metal in your mouth and is barely noticeable. You can remove the aligners to eat, drink, brush, and floss — making it the most lifestyle-compatible way to straighten your teeth.

How Clearalign Treatment Works

The Clearalign process is precise and personalised from start to finish.

  • Consultation & digital scan — We take a detailed 3D scan of your teeth to map the entire treatment plan
  • Custom aligner fabrication — Your full series of aligners is manufactured to exact specifications
  • Wearing your aligners — You wear each tray for 20–22 hours per day, only removing for meals and cleaning
  • Progress check-ins — Regular clinic visits every 6–8 weeks to monitor progress
  • Refinements if needed — Minor adjustments to the plan can be made mid-treatment if required
  • Retainer phase — After treatment, you wear a retainer at night to maintain your results

Is Clearalign Right for You?

Clearalign is highly effective for mild to moderate orthodontic cases and has advanced significantly — it can now treat many cases that previously required braces. The most important factor is your commitment to wearing the aligners for the required hours each day.

Clearalign is particularly popular with working professionals and adults who want to improve their smile without anyone knowing they are in treatment.

Key fact: Clearalign only works as well as your compliance. Wearing aligners for fewer than 20 hours per day significantly slows treatment and can compromise results. If you know you'll struggle with compliance, traditional braces may give you a more predictable outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

Treatment duration varies by case complexity. Simple cases: 6–9 months. Moderate cases: 12–18 months. More complex alignment issues: 18–24 months.

You should remove your aligners before eating and drinking anything other than plain water. Food and coloured drinks can stain and warp the trays.

Rinse them with cool water each time you remove them. Clean with a soft toothbrush and clear (not whitening) toothpaste morning and night. Never use hot water — it warps the plastic.

There may be a slight lisp for the first few days as your tongue adjusts to the new aligner. Most patients adapt completely within 1–2 weeks and speak normally.
